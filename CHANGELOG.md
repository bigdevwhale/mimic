## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- Postcards: share the year, the room, the mimic, a game or a creature you've met as a picture, in four looks; send it anywhere or save it to Pictures/Mimic.
- The chronicle in four tabs (overview, time, taste, pile) with much more in it: the year in days, play streaks, the average session, weekdays and time of day, the most played games, star ratings, new boxes by month and how fast the pile grows.
- A first-visit hint from the mimic on every screen, gone for good after «Got it».

### Changed
- A new look: a light 90s console, with the room on a dark screen and buttons in the colours of a pad. Pixels stay in the room and on its creatures; everything else uses the readable Nunito font.
- The bestiary creatures are drawn anew, larger and more detailed.
- Covers are shown as they are by default; pixel covers are still in the settings.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- A pixel room: shelves of boxes, the pile of unopened games, a TV with a wisp for each game in hands, a table with meeples, a rug and the mimic.
- Video games, board games and tabletop RPGs; catalogue search on Wikidata and Wikipedia with covers redrawn in pixels; box photos with the system camera.
- Popular genres for each kind, your own genres and subgenres with a colour and a trait.
- Notes on where you stopped, sessions with screenshots and camera photos, ratings and short reviews.
- The credits: the mimic eats the game and grows a trait from its genre; three stages from a little chest to an iron-bound one.
- "Mimic sniffs" and the die, the dig with a 30-minute trial or a ghost, lending with the courier crow.
- The chronicle (games and hours by month, genres, ratings, the pile), the bestiary, the real sky and weather in the window.
- English and Russian.
