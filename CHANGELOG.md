## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- A pixel room: shelves of boxes, the pile of unopened games, a TV with a wisp for each game in hands, a table with meeples, a rug and the mimic.
- Video games, board games and tabletop RPGs; catalogue search on Wikidata and Wikipedia with covers redrawn in pixels; box photos with the system camera.
- Popular genres for each kind, your own genres and subgenres with a colour and a trait.
- Notes on where you stopped, sessions with screenshots and camera photos, ratings and short reviews.
- The credits: the mimic eats the game and grows a trait from its genre; three stages from a little chest to an iron-bound one.
- "Mimic sniffs" and the die, the dig with a 30-minute trial or a ghost, lending with the courier crow.
- The chronicle (games and hours by month, genres, ratings, the pile), the bestiary, the real sky and weather in the window.
- English and Russian.
